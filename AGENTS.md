# Natural Human Copywriting & Anti-AI Standards (SEO & AEO Optimized)

All copy across user-facing pages, components, blog posts, guides, and metadata must adhere to authentic human editorial standards:

1. **No Em-Dashes or Semicolons**:
   - Never use em-dashes (`—`) or en-dashes (`–`) as conversational crutches or clause linkers.
   - Use clear, confident periods and natural commas instead.

2. **No AI Clichés or Promotional Buzzwords**:
   - Ban: `testament`, `delve`, `tapestry`, `monumental`, `bespoke`, `seamless`, `seamlessness`, `elevate`, `beacon`, `foster`, `groundbreaking`, `game-changing`, `living horology`, `pedagogical instrument`, `enduring legacy`, `journey through`.
   - Use direct, grounded, domain-accurate descriptions.

3. **Active Voice & Direct Cadence**:
   - Do NOT start sentences with gerund chains (e.g. avoid *"Synchronizing daily rituals...", "Showcasing Ujjain's heritage..."*).
   - Use direct subject-verb constructions (e.g. *"Tracks daily rituals...", "A permanent exhibit on Ujjain's zero meridian..."*).

4. **SEO & AEO Entity Preservation**:
   - Always retain authentic technical, geographical, and Sanskrit terminology (*Surya Siddhanta*, *Ujjain 0° prime meridian*, *Tithi*, *Nakshatra*, *Panchang*, *Muhurta*, *Ahoratra*, *Vikram Samvat*, *astronomical clock*).
   - Write clear declarative statements that Answer Engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Gemini) can cleanly extract and cite as facts.

5. **Natural Rhythm & Varied Sentence Length**:
   - Avoid artificial symmetry where every sentence has the same word count or identical clause structure.
   - Write like a knowledgeable museum curator, horological historian, or architectural editor.
